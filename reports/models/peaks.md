# Peak forecasts and high-peak warnings (M5)

Target: highest demand over the remaining hours of today, from each forecast issued after the day-ahead forecast is posted. High-peak event: that peak exceeds the 90th percentile of daily peaks for the calendar month in the two previous years (2025: 2023-2024; 2026: 2024-2025). Ensembles are stretched each month so the previous 90 days' 90% ranges would have caught 90%. M5 = M3 forecasts + 60 earlier days' whole-day error paths; R = the same method on Trick 2 (B4b); climatology = 2023-2024 event rate by month and issue hour over the same two years. Brier score: lower is better. p = DM on daily Brier differences, M5 vs R, Holm across BAs. Warnings: probability >= 50%.
