# Peak forecasts and high-peak warnings (M5)

Target: highest demand over the remaining hours of today, from each forecast issued after the day-ahead forecast is posted. High-peak event: that peak exceeds the 90th percentile of daily peaks for the calendar month in 2023-2024. M5 = M3 forecasts + 60 earlier days' whole-day error paths; R = the same method on Trick 2 (B4b); climatology = 2023-2024 event rate by month and issue hour. Brier score: lower is better. p = DM on daily Brier differences, M5 vs R, Holm across BAs. Warnings: probability >= 50%.
